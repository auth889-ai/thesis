## The three sentences that win the room
1. "You can never replay the same moment twice on a real road — so I built the
   machine that can, and froze the moment down to the randomness."
2. "My system sometimes refuses to act — a scripted demo can't do that."
3. "Every number on every slide regenerates from one command; ask me to run any of them."

## Hard questions → answers
- "Is SUMO not just a game?" → It is the standard instrument of the field —
  IntelliLight, PressLight, CoLight, RESCO all prove results in it; my twin is
  synchronized to a real feed and GEH-checked, which they don't do.
- "Why no real signal control?" → SURTRAC/CIRCLES had authority/vehicle access;
  my capability modes make the same architecture deployable the day a city
  grants access — and nothing is claimed before that.
- "Why did FA-MAPPO not finish?" → infrastructure crash, reported honestly;
  the trained arms and the negative results are in the matrix.
- "Is the camera Dhaka?" → No — it is a real US DOT feed, declared as such;
  Dhaka cameras are registered the moment a real feed exists. The perception
  stack is Dhaka-trained (rickshaw/CNG) and the twin is the real Dhaka network.
