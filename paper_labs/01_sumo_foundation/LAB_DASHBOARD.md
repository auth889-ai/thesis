## Results — 5 seeds × 3 controllers × 2100 s, identical seeded demand (FROZEN)

| Controller | Throughput | Avg TT (s) | p95 TT (s) | Avg wait (s) | Mean queue | p95 queue | Spillback proxy |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Fixed-Time | 757.8 | 140.6 ± 6.4 | 241.6 | 63.3 | 23.7 | 38.0 | **0.41** |
| Queue-Actuated | 739.2 | 147.0 ± 3.9 | 242.6 | **57.9** | **21.4** | **32.2** | **0.00** |
| Max-Pressure\* | 732.2 | 149.7 ± 4.9 | 246.9 | 59.2 | 21.7 | **32.2** | **0.00** |

\* `BASELINE_IMPLEMENTATION`, single junction — **not** a Varaiya network reproduction.

**Honest reading:** adaptive control cuts waiting (−8.5 % / −6.5 %), mean queues
(−10 %) and p95 queues (−15 %), and removes the spillback-proxy episodes that
fixed-time hits in 41 % of steps; fixed-time keeps a small throughput and
travel-time edge at this single symmetric junction. No controller is declared a
general winner.

## Safety & verification

- **0 collisions, 0 teleports across 15/15 runs** (`verification_record.json`).
- Yellow always traversed: greens are terminated with `setPhaseDuration(0)`
  against a program whose phase list contains `yyrr`/`rryy`; asserted by
  `tests/unit/test_scenario_xml.py`; visualised in `FIG/10`.
- Min/max-green enforced as pre-decision guards in both adaptive controllers.
- 2 isolated emergency-braking warnings across all runs (recorded, not hidden).
- Independent recomputation of 4 metrics × 3 controllers → **exact match**.
- Bologna: `exit_code=0`, `tracked_files_modified=no`.

## Limitations (carried forward)

1. Max-Pressure is single-intersection; network-scale benchmark deferred.
2. Stops = `waitingCount` proxy; spillback = queue-ratio proxy (both labelled).
3. Bologna used only as an execution check — the scenario itself is documented
   as ~30 usable minutes, small, no pedestrians, unrealistic multi-lane
   roundabouts, and its authors advise against rerouting studies with it.
4. Unit scenario is synthetic and symmetric; results do not generalise.
5. Heterogeneous traffic (SUMO sublane model) identified but **not** exercised.
6. First Bologna attempt failed (exit 127, `/usr/bin/time` absent in Colab) —
   our wrapper was fixed and re-run; both states are recorded.
