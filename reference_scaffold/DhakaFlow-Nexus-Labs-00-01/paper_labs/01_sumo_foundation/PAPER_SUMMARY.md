# PAPER_SUMMARY — SUMO canonical paper

SUMO is a microscopic traffic simulation framework: individual vehicles, lanes, connections, right-of-way, signals and movement dynamics are represented explicitly. The paper's workflow requires road network + traffic infrastructure + demand to form a scenario, and emphasizes qualitative GUI validation plus quantitative outputs.

The paper's real-world Bologna scenario includes real traffic signal plans and traffic demand derived from induction-loop measurements. That scenario is the official real-data anchor for this lab.
